### Mascara
It enhances the eyelashes. Consequently and inevitably, it enhances the eyes (because of the proximity, I presume). Arguably, it's made for it. To highlight the eyes is the end goal.

### Eye
The eye is the entrance to the soul. It is also the entrance to the worldview. Beauty is visual. You know beauty when you see it. Therefore, the eye is the entrance to beauty. 

There is inner beauty and outer beauty. Generally, when we say beauty, we refer to outer beauty. Why? Because you can only literally "see" outer beauty, and you can only literally "sense"/"feel" inner beauty. "See" is more straightforward. And people like things that are easy to understand because they are self-affirming. Ultimately, we want to achieve both. They're complementary. In an application sense, this is achieved through UI and UX.

### Face[^1]
Human is so good at recognising faces because we've been trained to do that since we're a baby. This is because when you're a baby, your visual scope is so short that you can only see things directly in front of you; faces. And you needed to remember at least your mom's face because she's your life source. In fact, we have an area in our brain dedicated to recognising faces. This is also not limited to human faces; we even see faces when there isn't a literal face.
