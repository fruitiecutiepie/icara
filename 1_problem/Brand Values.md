# Brand Values

What Icara is:
1. Personalised. Icara is made for you, and you only. Therefore, it wants you to win. Not win itself. It helps you understand yourself better. It revolves around you.
2. Brand-agnostic. Ingredients (first principles) are the king.
3. Natural. It wants to enhance you, not modify/alter you. It believes in nature.
4. Reliable. All information is accurate. If it's a grey matter, you will know about it.

What Icara is not:
1. Community-focused. It acknowledges the world around you exists, but it's not made for that. It doesn't focus on the world.
2. Evangelise certain beauty paradigms. If it works for you, it works for you. But it wants to help you figure out what will work best for you, i.e., if what works can be improved. You are the source of truth.

## Principles/Strategies

1. Don't make it tedious. Make it simple. Make it fun.
2. We're dealing with the visual industry here, just like every other art industry. (Also, we are a lifestyle brand. In the needs hierarchy, it's probably on the secondary/tertiary level. But also arguably not too above either, considering [[Historical Practices]]). Therefore, Icara has to be visually/aesthetically pleasing to look at and use.
