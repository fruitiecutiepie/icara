# Business Plan

Business model: subscription, $3.99/month. $29.99/year or $2.49/month, billed annually. free trial 7 days? possibly add in-app purchases for add-on static features. OR. we can just make it a paid app for $7.99 bcs we don't want to maintain it for years to come. and we can still have in-app purchases.

In-app purchases: themes (like LINE)

> Question to ask when deciding whether a feature should be premium:
> Do regular (most) people, presumably with a relatively small collection, need/care about this?

Free-tier users will not be able to:
1. Add > 1 purchase for each item or repurchase item -> no sense of history
2. Add > 1 variant for each item
3. Add > 1 photo for each variant
