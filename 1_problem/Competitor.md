# Competitor

THERE IS NO NATIVE DESKTOP OR WEB APP MADE FOR BEAUTY APPS.

I'm getting more and more convinced that a desktop or at least a web app is needed for my app. Because phone's screen real estate is too small. And it's annoying to put all your items from those little screens. I don't like phones either. I don't use them much. Their advantage is mostly just their portability. The apps on my phone only have to have features that I would be interested in on checking on the go. When I work, I go to my laptop. That means, my working apps are on my laptop. That means, if your app involves working, make sure it works on laptops.
