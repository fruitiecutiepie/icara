# References

## Cosmetics Ingredients
1. [CosIng - Cosmetics - GROWTH - European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/growth/tools-databases/cosing/)
	1. Cosing is the European Commission database for information on cosmetic substances and ingredients.
	2. Services:
		1. Search which ingredients have which functions. E.g., Zinc oxide -> antimicrobial, bulking, colorant, oral care, etc.
		2. Functions definitions
2. [Cosmetic Ingredient Review |](https://www.cir-safety.org/)
3. [INCIDecoder - Decode your skincare ingredients](https://incidecoder.com/)
	1. They're probably a PCPC member
	2. Uses CosIng to determine ingredient functions, but *doesn't* copy them exactly. Their information is also not the same as SkinSort.
	3. Services:
		1. Copy and paste ingredient lists and they'll tell you all the details
		2. Search for products -> work cross-way from ingredients -> recursive
		3. Embed ingredient lists with short explanations, the product has to be known/exist, but because we can just copy-paste ingredients, this site is easier to scrape and provides more modular useful details than SkinSort
5. [SkinSort](https://skinsort.com/)
	1. SkinSort uses CosIng to source *some* of its data on ingredient names and functions, they *don't* copy them exactly. Their information is also not the same as INCIDecoder.
	2. They're probably a PCPC member
	3. Services:
		1. Embed ingredient lists with features (not short explanations), the product has to be known/exist -> [Ingredient widgets](https://skinsort.com/widgets)
6. [Personal Care Products Council](https://www.personalcarecouncil.org/)
	1. Services:
		1. [INCIpedia](https://incipedia.personalcarecouncil.org/)
			1. **INCI®** is a registered trademark of the Personal Care Products Council (PCPC). INCI is a list of the standardized and internationally accepted names used in the declaration of ingredients on cosmetic and personal care products.
		2. [Subscriptions](https://incipedia.personalcarecouncil.org/subscriptions/)
		3. [Cosmetic Ingredient Review](https://www.cir-safety.org/)
7. [Cosmetics Europe - The Personal Care Association](https://cosmeticseurope.eu/)
	1. Services:
		1. [Understanding the Label](https://cosmeticseurope.eu/cosmetic-products/understanding-label/)
		2. [Cosmetic Ingredient Database - COSMILE Europe](https://cosmileeurope.eu/inci/), contains ingredient functions and their definitions, got these from CosIng as well, copy them *exactly*.
8. [EWG Skin Deep® Cosmetics Database](https://www.ewg.org/skindeep/)

## World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)
The World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) is the global forum for intellectual property (IP) services, policy, information and cooperation. We are a self-funding agency of the United Nations, with 193 member states.

Services:
1. Patents
	1. PCT - The International Patent System
	2. PATENTSCOPE
2. Trademarks
	1. Madrid - The International Trademark System
		1. The Madrid System is a convenient and cost-effective solution for registering and managing [trademarks](https://www.wipo.int/trademarks/en/) worldwide. File a single international trademark application and [pay one set of fees](https://www.wipo.int/finance/en/madrid.html) to apply for protection in up to [130 countries](https://www.wipo.int/madrid/en/members/). Modify, renew or expand your global trademark portfolio through one centralized system.

6. [Nice Classification](https://www.wipo.int/classifications/nice/nclpub/)
	1. It is an international classification system used to classify goods and services for the purposes of the registration of marks.

	3. Cosmetics fall into Class 3, but brushes/makeup tools fall under Class 21
	4. Other tools: [Classification Assistance - TMclass](http://tmclass.tmdn.org/ec2/)
